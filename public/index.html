Connect your PayPal sandbox

This hosted desk runs on your own sandbox app and your own Gemini key. Nothing is saved in this browser: the fields live in this page only while it is open, and the desk holds them in its memory for this session. Connect checks both keys first: PayPal with a real API call, Gemini with a free model-list read. A rejected pair or key fails right here; if Google cannot be reached for that read, the Gemini key is confirmed on the first decision instead.

The session ends after 30 minutes idle, and after 4 hours even if you keep working. It also ends when the desk restarts. Disconnect discards your keys and the session cookie — if a charge or decision is still running at that moment, the desk finishes it first, then discards them.
